NOWA / Our process

HOW WE WORK.

A deliberate five-stage system that gives exploration room to breathe while keeping delivery visible and controlled.

↓
DISCOVER / DEFINE / DESIGN / BUILD / GROW

01 Five stages

A process that keeps decisions connected.

Each stage reduces a different kind of uncertainty. We move forward when the next set of decisions is clear enough to act on.

01

Discover

Understand the real problem.

Goals, users, context, constraints and success criteria.

  • Kickoff
  • Research
  • Audit
  • Stakeholder alignment
01
02

Define

Turn information into direction.

Priorities, scope, journeys, architecture and a workable product plan.

  • MVP scope
  • User flows
  • Content model
  • Roadmap
02
03

Design

Make the experience tangible.

Structure becomes interface, system, prototype and motion behavior.

  • Wireframes
  • UI direction
  • Design system
  • Prototype
03
04

Build

Engineer the system behind the interface.

Development, integration, testing, performance and deployment.

  • Frontend
  • Backend
  • QA
  • Deployment
04
05

Grow

Learn from real use.

Launch support, feedback loops, improvements and ongoing product evolution.

  • Analytics
  • Iteration
  • Optimisation
  • Support
05

02 Not a waterfall

The stages connect in a loop.

Feedback can move a decision backward before it becomes expensive. Learning after launch moves the product forward again.

03 What stays visible

Every stage leaves something useful behind.

01

Decisions

Why we chose a direction and what it solves.

02

Next steps

04 Working principles

How we keep the process useful.

01

Show work early.

Useful feedback arrives sooner when the thing is visible.

02

Document decisions.

Context should survive meetings and handoffs.

03

Reduce moving parts.

Complexity is a cost. We add it only when the product earns it.

04

Protect momentum.

Small, clear decisions keep teams moving better than giant reveal moments.
